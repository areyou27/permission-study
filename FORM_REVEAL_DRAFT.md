## Part 2: removing StudySpot's permissions

> **Needs the teacher's OK before it goes in the form.** This text is new. It is not part of Appendix G.

**If you tapped Allow, here is how to take it back**

StudySpot was a web page, so anything you allowed applies only to that page's website, `areyou27.github.io`. It is not an app on your phone, and it does not appear in your phone's list of apps. You can remove what you allowed in under a minute.

**iPhone (Safari)**

1. Open the StudySpot link again in Safari. You do not need to tap Start.
2. Tap the page-settings icon in the address bar (it may look like **aA**), then **Website Settings**.
3. Set Location, Camera, and Microphone to **Deny** or **Ask**.
4. Or open the Settings app, then Apps, then Safari, then Location, Camera, or Microphone, and remove **areyou27.github.io**.

**Android (Chrome)**

1. Open the StudySpot link again in Chrome. You do not need to tap Start.
2. Tap the lock or tune icon to the left of the address, then **Permissions**.
3. Tap **Reset permissions**, or switch each one off.
4. Or open Chrome's Settings, then Site settings, and find **areyou27.github.io**.

If you tapped Don't Allow each time, there is nothing to remove.
